# STAR-Prepper: The Behavioural Cookbook

The behavioural (or competency-based) interview is a very common procedure across many industries, and the [STAR method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation,_task,_action,_result) has long been the go-to technique for succeeding at it.

## What is STAR-Prepper?

STAR-Prepper is an online repository for storing your collection of stories, along with their sub-stories, allowing you to link each sub-story with its corresponding competencies. For example, your team project at university might have taught you teamwork, leadership, and/or conflict resolution. You decide what you learnt from each version of the story.

Also, you can practise interviewing on STAR-Prepper:
- List out the core competencies from a given job description or company mission page
- STAR-Prepper will conduct a timed mock interview with relevant questions

In essence, STAR-Prepper is the tool you use to not only organise your past experiences and stories, but also practise verbalising them.
